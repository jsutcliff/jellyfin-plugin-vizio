Pair with TV

Find it on the TV under Network settings. The TV must be on.

Launch link

Open or bookmark this on your phone to start Jellyfin on the TV. It only works from your local network.

The launch link signs the TV in as this user, so there's no login screen. Anyone on your network who opens the launch link can start the TV signed in as them.
Leave empty to use the address the launch link was opened with, plus /web/.
Writes the TV's key presses to the Jellyfin server log.
